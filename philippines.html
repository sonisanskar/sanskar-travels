Day by Day

The Full Itinerary

Every stop, every cost, exactly how it happened.

Day 1 · 17–18 Oct

Mumbai → Singapore → Cebu

  • Took the 11:30 pm flight out of Mumbai, landing in Singapore at 7 am, then onward to Cebu by 3 pm
  • Booked two months ahead on Singapore Airlines — round trip for two ₹52,000 total
  • Stayed at Amaris Bed & Breakfast, about 10 minutes from Cebu airport — chosen purely for proximity, since the next day's Coron flight left in the afternoon
  • Skipped Moalboal, which is where most travelers start their first leg
  • Evening wandering the local neighbourhood and the mall; dinner at a café right in front of our stay

Somewhere above the clouds on the way to Cebu.

Day 2 · 19 Oct

Fly to Coron & Mount Tapyas Sunset

  • Breakfast at the hotel, then the 1:40 pm flight from Cebu to Coron
  • Checked into Sunz En Coron Resort — a luxury stay, with the airport pickup arranged by the resort (about an hour's drive)
  • Climbed Mount Tapyas — roughly 720+ steps — for a sunset over the whole town and bay; completely worth it
  • Booked the Coron Ultimate Tour through Experience Expedition for the next day ₱1,900/person
  • Booked the Coron → El Nido ferry on arrival ~₱3,000/person — book early and check the schedule, as when we went it only ran on Thursdays
  • Dinner at Verde Rekado, a fully vegetarian restaurant — a 10/10 spot, and one of the best places in town for a sunset with good food

At the Mount Tapyas viewpoint over Coron Bay.

Day 3 · 20 Oct

The Coron Ultimate Tour

  • Pickup at 8:30 am for the Coron Ultimate Tour — the best way to see Coron's islands in one day
  • Snorkeling and kayaking, plus a dip into Barracuda Lake
  • The Twin Lagoon was mesmerising
  • Fed the fish at the Skeleton Wreck

Limestone walls and glass-clear water on the Coron Ultimate Tour.

Day 4 · 21 Oct

Island Escapade Tour

  • Pickup at 8 am for the Island Escapade Tour, which hits three stunning beaches
  • Malcapuya Island was the standout of the three
  • The usual stops (varies by operator): Malcapuya Island, whose white sand is often compared to Boracay's; Bulog Dos, a sandbar that emerges at low tide; and Coco Beach, with hammocks and cottages for lazing around. Some operators swap in Banana Island (a crescent beach with coral snorkeling) or Ditaytayan sandbar (shallow water and views)
  • Time at each stop varies — some groups linger at Coco Beach and get less time at Bulog Dos, so ask your operator about the split
Day 5 · 22 Oct

Ferry to El Nido

  • 6 am ferry from Coron to El Nido — about 5 hours on the water
  • Checked into Coco Resort, a lovely spot right on Corong-Corong Beach
  • Caught the sunset from the beach, then headed into town for dinner
  • Booked Island Tour A for the next day — 7 Commando Beach, Shimizu Island, the Big and Small Lagoons, and the Secret Lagoon
  • Where to eat: Bellavita for the best pizza in town (right next door to Coco Resort), Verde for the best vegan food, and Happiness Beach Bar for the best Mediterranean
Day 6 · 23 Oct

El Nido Island Tour A

  • Pickup at 8:30 am; back around 5 pm
  • Bring aqua shoes with you — the rocky entries to the lagoons are much easier with them
  • Tour A stops: Big Lagoon, Secret Lagoon, Shimizu Island and 7 Commando Beach, with Payong-Payong Beach turning up on some runs. The order changes by operator and day, as boats reshuffle to dodge crowds
  • Small Lagoon was on our tour, but some operators have since moved it over to Tour D — worth checking if it's on your must-see list

A narrow passage between limestone cliffs on the El Nido island tour.

Day 7 · 24 Oct

A Proper Rest Day

  • Nothing planned — a deliberate rest day after back-to-back boat tours
  • Sunset at Las Cabanas Beach

Dusk on the beach at El Nido, on the rest day.

Day 8 · 25 Oct

The Buffer Day

  • Kept free on purpose — it was retreating monsoon season, so tours could easily get cancelled or delayed
  • Use it either for Nacpan Beach or for Island Tour C
  • Tour C stops: Helicopter Island, Matinloc Shrine (one operator lists a ~₱200 entrance fee), Secret Beach and Hidden Beach. Some operators add Star Beach or Tapiutan Island, and the day runs roughly 6–9 hours depending on the operator
Day 9 · 26 Oct

Back to Cebu

  • Afternoon flight from El Nido back to Cebu
  • Reached in the evening and headed to the city centre for dinner
Day 10 · 27 Oct

Cebu Churches & a Singapore Stopover

  • Morning visiting Cebu's churches before the flight out
  • Landed in Singapore around 7 pm for a 24-hour stopover
  • Went straight to the Spectra light and water show at 9 pm
  • Booked a night at a Spacepod in Lavender
  • Dinner at Lau Pa Sat
Day 11 · 28 Oct

Gardens by the Bay & the Flight Home

  • Morning at Gardens by the Bay
  • Haji Lane for its colourful lanes; lunch at Babylon Cafe Bar and Grill
  • Flight back to India

💡 Travel Tips

  • Book flights early — we locked ours two months ahead to get the best Singapore Airlines fare.
  • Book the Coron → El Nido ferry in advance and check the schedule; it doesn't sail every day.
  • Pack aqua shoes for the El Nido lagoons.
  • Build in a buffer day if you're travelling in retreating monsoon season (October) — tours get cancelled for weather.
  • Indian passport holders can currently enter visa-free for up to 14 days (non-extendable), but you'll need confirmed accommodation, proof of funds, an onward ticket and an eTravel registration — confirm the latest rules with the Philippine Embassy before you fly.
  • Singapore stopover — visa: Indian passport holders need an approved visa before stepping out of the airport on a stopover; there's no visa-on-arrival or waiver, and the Singapore mission in Mumbai lists visa applications as required for Indian passport holders. Expect a passport valid 6+ months, a recent photo, and the application form, with typical processing of about 3–5 working days — so apply well ahead.
  • Singapore visa fee: sources disagree — roughly SGD 30 in some guides, about ₹2,100 per application in another citing the High Commission, plus agent charges if you use one. The fee is non-refundable even if the application is refused.
  • SG Arrival Card: separate from the visa and free — submit it online through the official ICA system shortly before you land, and carry your passport and a printout of the e-visa to immigration.
  • Transit-only: guides conflict on whether Indians can transit Changi without a visa, and one ties the 96-hour facility to holding a US/UK/Japan-type visa. If you won't leave the airport, confirm with ICA or the High Commission rather than trusting a blog — and note that a 24-hour stopover with a night out means you'll want the visa.
  • Vegetarians and vegans are well served in Coron and El Nido — Verde Rekado and Verde are both excellent.

✨ Extra Recos I'd Add

  • Kayangan Lake (Coron) — a staple of Coron's island-hopping circuit and worth confirming is on your tour.
  • Maquinit Hot Spring (Coron) — a saltwater hot spring that's a great way to wind down after a tour day.
  • AlFresco Coron — a rooftop with a good bay view for sunset, and vegan options on the menu.
  • Island Tour C (El Nido) — Secret Beach, Hidden Beach and Matinloc Shrine, with some of the best snorkeling; guides rate A and C as the top two tours.
  • Nacpan Beach (El Nido) — a long, quieter stretch of sand that makes a great buffer-day trip.
  • Moalboal (Cebu) — skipped on this trip, but a classic add-on for snorkeling if you have a spare day at the start.
