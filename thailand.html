Day by Day

The Full Itinerary

Every stop, every cost, exactly how it happened.

Day 1 · 29 Nov

Arrival in Phuket & Patong Beach

  • Landed around 10–11 am; took an airport shuttle to Patong Beach ~100 THB
  • Patong is the liveliest strip — Bangla Street for nightlife, and 7-Elevens everywhere for easy vegetarian snacking
  • Checked in and rested, then explored in the evening once the afternoon heat eased off
  • Wandered the night market and the Patong Beach area
  • Dinner at Euro Thai restaurant — authentic Thai cuisine

First evening on Patong Beach.

Day 2 · 30 Nov

Big Buddha, Chalong Temple & Old Town

  • Started around 10 am; visited the Big Buddha statue — the front was closed for landscaping but the back viewpoint was still worth it
  • Rented bikes for the day ~250 THB — carry an International Driving Permit (IDP), or local police will fine you
  • Stopped at Kata Beach, then Chalong Temple — a scenic must-see
  • Back to Kata Beach for sunset; meals for the day were mostly 7-Eleven runs
  • Evening: Sunday walking street market in Old Town, known for local crafts, plus the colourful "Soi Romanee" street
  • Booked a Phi Phi island tour for the next day — bargained a group of 10–11 people down to 1200 THB/person

Taking in the view from the Big Buddha hilltop.

Day 3 · 1 Dec

Phi Phi Islands Tour

  • Pickup at 8 am (included in the tour) to the pier, breakfast, then the island-hopping tour began
  • Covered Maya Bay (the most beautiful stop), Monkey Beach, Bamboo/Coral viewpoints and a lagoon viewpoint
  • Took a long-tail boat into the lagoon for photos ~300–400 THB/person extra
  • Lunch on Phi Phi island, included in the tour package
  • Back at the pier by 5:30 pm; Indian dinner at Amritsar restaurant back in Patong
  • Booked the ferry from Phuket to Koh Samui for the next leg ~800 THB

Maya Bay — the most beautiful stop on the tour.

Day 4 · 2 Dec

Transfer to Koh Samui

  • Cab to the pier at 8 am, ferry to Koh Samui, arriving around 4 pm — transport was all part of the 800 THB ticket
  • Minivan/tuk-tuk onward to Chaweng Beach — one of the most happening areas, packed with cafés
  • Evening exploring Chaweng and trying local cuisine; dinner at Thai Spices restaurant

Catching the sunset on the ferry over to Koh Samui.

Day 5 · 3 Dec

Chill Day at the Beach

  • A slow, no-plans day — stayed at a resort with a private beach right on Chaweng
  • Beach games and lounging all day — Koh Samui is the place to actually relax with far fewer crowds

Bean bags and a coastline view — the Koh Samui pace.

Day 6 · 4 Dec

Snorkeling & the Full Moon Party

  • Group split: one half went snorkeling at Koh Tao ~1400 THB, the other to Coral Island for reef snorkeling
  • Booked the Full Moon Party package for the evening 2500 THB
  • Ferry to Koh Phangan at 9 pm for the party, which ran until around 4 am
  • Back to the stay in Koh Samui by 6 am
Day 7 · 5 Dec

Departure via Surat Thani

  • Transfer booked from Koh Samui to Surat Thani for the flight home
  • Tip: skip the combined booking — take the Raja Ferry pier directly and buy a ticket straight through to Surat Thani airport ~700–800 THB

💡 Travel Tips

  • Renting a bike? Carry an International Driving Permit (IDP) — local police actively fine tourists without one.
  • Look for the "ล" and reversed "J"-style vegetarian symbol on food stalls and menus.
  • Bargain as much as possible, especially on group tours and markets.
  • Pack dry Indian snacks — theplas and khakras travel best.
  • Carry a forex card plus a decent amount of cash.
  • Thailand is visa-free for Indians — just fill out the TDAC form online before arrival.
  • Avoid metered taxis for large groups — they end up costing about the same as private transport.
