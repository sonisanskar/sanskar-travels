Day by Day

The Full Itinerary

A journey through majestic Oman — every stop, every cost.

Day 1 · 22 Dec

Arrival in Muscat

  • Landed in Muscat around midnight; rented a car directly at the airport (Europcar, Dollar & Alamo all recommended — book a 4x4 in advance if heading to Jebel Akhdar or Wahiba Sands)
  • We took a Nissan X-Trail from Dollar for 7 days 320 OMR total — 166 OMR rent, 70 OMR full insurance, 75 OMR deposit
  • Checked into Radisson Hotel, Al Khuwair for 2 nights ~25,000 INR / 5 people, near Sultan Qaboos Mosque & the Royal Opera House
  • Drove to Seeb, snorkeling day tour to Al Damaniyat Islands with Pearl Damaniyat 23 OMR
  • Lunch at Mall of Muscat — paneer & veg handi biryani 8 OMR; grocery run at Lulu Supermarket
  • Sunset at Muttrah Fort 3 OMR entry, then browsed Muttrah Souk (bargaining recommended)
  • Dinner at Saravana Bhawan ~10 OMR

Muttrah at sunset — old Muscat framed by the mountains.

Day 2 · 23 Dec

Exploration & Adventures

  • Morning visit to Sultan Qaboos Mosque — free entry for tourists, 8:30–11:30 am
  • 2-hour drive to Bimmah Sinkhole, then on to Wadi Ash Shab — a mini Grand Canyon of towering cliffs and turquoise pools
  • Boat ride 1 OMR + life jacket 1 OMR to reach the trek/swim to the cave; arrived 12:30 pm, left reluctantly by 5 pm
  • Coffee stop en route to Wahiba Sands; stayed at Dome Stays (Bubble Dome) 100 OMR / 2 rooms — sunrise from bed, unbeatable

The trek and swim into Wadi Ash Shab's hidden pools.

Day 3 · 24 Dec

Thrills in the Desert

  • Morning dune bashing 30 OMR/car and ATV riding 7 OMR / 20 min — bargain hard
  • Stopped at Kafka Café en route to Nizwa; detour to Ibra town & the ruins of Minzifah
  • Reached Nizwa by evening, stayed at Al Diyar Hotel 27 OMR / 5 people
  • Chai & snacks at Karak 24/7, shopping at Nizwa Souk
Day 4 · 25 Dec

Historical Insights

  • Morning at Bahla Fort 4 OMR/person, lunch nearby at Wahad Al Tabiya
  • Explored Jibreen Castle 3.15 OMR/person, caught a stunning sunset
  • Rose latte & cheesecake at Qadam Café; dinner at Karak 24/7

The fort at sunset, ringed by a palm oasis.

Day 5 · 26 Dec

Mountains and Views

  • Late start to Jebel Akhdar, ~1 hr from Nizwa — a 4x4 is essential to clear the checkpost
  • Reached the top by afternoon, short trek towards Wadi Bin Khalid
  • Sunset with coffee at WN Café on the way back
  • Based out of Nizwa for all three mountain/desert days
Day 6 · 27 Dec

Cultural Experiences

  • Early Friday morning at the famous Nizwa goat auction, Bisya Souq
  • Browsed Nizwa Souq for Omani halwa; skipped Nizwa Fort (pricey, low interest)
  • Afternoon drive back to Muscat, quick lunch at McDonald's
  • Brief stop at Al Hinn Sulphur Springs; sunset at Al Qurum Beach
  • Stayed at Al Haffa Hotel 45 OMR / 2 rooms, 5 people — pool, gym & breakfast included
  • Dinner at Kamath's, Indian delicacies under 17 OMR
  • Muscat Flower Festival — a million flowers 2 OMR, cards only

Sunset at Al Qurum Beach, back in Muscat.

Day 7 · 28 Dec

Departure and Final Exploration

  • Checked out ahead of a night flight home; breakfast at the hotel
  • Royal Opera House visit — check timings, open 1–3 pm
  • Morning shopping run at Lulu Hypermarket for chocolates, chips & date packets
  • Lunch at Saravana Bhavan (the go-to South Indian chain here)
  • Al Amin Mosque access was restricted on Saturdays — plan ahead
  • Sunset drive to Bandar Al Khairan, ~1 hr from Muscat, plus a scenic boat ride 15 OMR
  • Farewell dinner at Roznah, a boutique palace-style restaurant, before heading to the airport around 8 pm

💡 Travel Tips

  • Book your rental car — especially 4x4s — well in advance; availability is limited.
  • A 4x4 is non-negotiable for Jebel Akhdar and Wahiba Sands.
  • Bargain at desert camps for dune bashing and ATV rides.
  • Carry cash (OMR) — some attractions like the Flower Festival are card-only, others are cash-only.
  • Base out of one town (e.g. Nizwa) for a few days to avoid repacking every night.

💰 Trip Cost Summary

What the itinerary adds up to, per person, assuming a group of 4–5 sharing the rental car and rooms — flight cost sourced from current India–Muscat fare averages since it wasn't in the original diary.

₹60,500 – ₹82,500 per person, all-in (~$730–$995)
Flights (round-trip, India ↔ Muscat)₹27,500 – ₹38,500
Stays (6 nights, split across group)₹6,600 – ₹9,900
Car rental + fuel (split across group)₹3,300 – ₹5,000
Activities (snorkeling, dune bashing, ATV, forts, boat rides)₹9,900 – ₹13,200
Food₹6,600 – ₹8,800
Visa & misc₹4,400 – ₹5,500

Approximate figures for 2026 — solo travelers should budget notably higher since the car rental and rooms won't be split. Going with 3–5 friends (like this trip) is what keeps Oman surprisingly affordable despite the pricier flights.
