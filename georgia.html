Day by Day

The Full Itinerary

Every stop, every cost, exactly how it happened.

Day 1

Arrival & the Drive to Gudauri

  • Landed in the afternoon; picked up a car pre-booked through Local Rent online — better rates than booking on arrival
  • Bought a Magti SIM at the airport — best network coverage countrywide; avoid Silknet. A ₹1500–2000 plan gets you unlimited data
  • Started the drive north to Gudauri at 3 pm, stopping en route at Ananuri Fortress
  • Checked into a stay at Gudauri Ski Resort; dinner at Haveli restaurant
  • Carried USD from home and exchanged it in bigger cities like Tbilisi, Kutaisi, or Batumi for better rates
Day 2

Paragliding, Truso Valley & Gergeti Trinity Church

  • Started at 7 am, passing the Russia–Georgia Friendship Monument on the way north
  • Trek to Truso Valley — book a taxi at the start to cover the first 5 km, then trek onward to the monastery (3–4 hours round trip)
  • After returning from the hike, went paragliding in the afternoon ~300 GEL/person
  • Drove into the Kazbegi region up to Gergeti Trinity Church, catching the sunset there
  • Back to our stay in Kazbegi; dinner at a local restaurant
Day 3

The Juta Valley Trek

  • Started at 7 am; drove to the entry point, then took a taxi to the trailhead ~10 GEL/person
  • Trekked the Juta Valley (Chaukhi Pass) — 7 to 10 hours round trip to the top and back; a shorter ~3-hour option turns back at the lake
  • The trail is well-marked and doable without a guide — just follow the other trekkers
  • Back to our stay in the Kazbegi area for the night

A hammock break partway up the Juta Valley trail.

Day 4

On to Kutaisi

  • A chill start after the previous day's trek — left around 11 am for Kutaisi
  • River rafting en route ~350 GEL
  • Stopped at Mtskheta to see Svetitskhoveli Cathedral — genuinely beautiful
  • Dinner en route, reaching Kutaisi by 9–10 pm
  • Explored Kutaisi's nightlife on foot that same night — club hopping and cafes; the scene here is surprisingly good

Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, Mtskheta — genuinely beautiful.

Day 5

Canyons, Caves & the Drive to Batumi

  • Morning drive toward Batumi via Okatse Canyon, Martvili Canyon, and the Prometheus Caves
  • Reached the stay in Batumi by around 8:50 pm; dinner at Spice Garden
  • Spent much of the night at the casino — plenty of options in Batumi
  • Worth flagging: we ran into some unwelcoming treatment and poor reviews from a few Airbnb hosts during this leg — hosting norms for Indian guests can differ from what you'd expect back home, so it's worth being prepared for it

Inside the Prometheus Caves, lit up in shifting colour.

Day 6

Exploring Batumi

  • Started around 9–10 am; explored the city on foot and by rental scooter — parking a car in Batumi's old town is genuinely difficult
  • Piazza Square, European Square, and the city's art museum; lunch at Piazza Square and later at Sheraton
  • Walked the length of Batumi Boulevard to the Ali and Nino moving statue, in front of the Ferris wheel
  • Booked a private yacht for an hour on the water — music, dancing, thoroughly recommended
  • Rode the Ferris wheel afterward — also recommended; go at sunset if you can get a slot
  • Visited the Batumi Botanical Garden's Japanese-style garden
  • Another night at the casino to close out Batumi

Golden hour on the private yacht ride — the highlight of Batumi.

Day 7

Batumi to Tbilisi

  • Left Batumi around 11 am, aiming to stop at a cave city en route — didn't work out due to some last-minute hiccups
  • Drove straight through to Tbilisi, arriving around 8 pm
Day 8

Tbilisi Old Town & the Hop-On Hop-Off Tour

  • Stayed in the Old Town, walking distance from most attractions
  • Morning walk/run along the riverfront
  • Explored on foot — souvenir shopping, good coffee at Craft Coffee Shop and Daily Grind Coffee
  • Booked a 24-hour hop-on hop-off bus ~50 GEL — fully recommended
  • Took the cable car up to the Mother of Georgia statue, and passed old European architecture and the grand railway terminus among the stops

The whole crew, overlooking Tbilisi from Narikala.

Day 9

Sulfur Baths & a Final Tbilisi Evening

  • A completely chill morning at the Abanotubani sulfur baths — sauna, hot pool, and a cold plunge, plus a booked scrub-and-massage session; fully worth it
  • Lunch at a Thai place near Sioni Church
  • Evening at the Clock Tower — get a portrait printed on a newspaper page, catch live music at Muse, and get a caricature drawn on your coffee cup at Kvartz Coffee
  • Dinner at a Mexican restaurant, Fiesta
Day 10

Departure

  • Flight home to Delhi via Doha on Qatar Airways, connecting onward on IndiGo

💡 Travel Tips

  • Bargain wherever you can — flea markets and local shops expect it.
  • Most people in Georgia don't speak English — keep a translation app handy.
  • Bring USD from home and exchange at local currency shops, not the airport — you'll get close to the exact rate with no commission.
  • If you've rented a car, consider dropping it off before Batumi/Tbilisi city exploring — narrow streets make parking a real hassle.
  • Local shops and flea markets generally accept cards, so a forex card works fine even off the beaten path.
  • Pack sufficient dry snacks — vegetarian options are limited. Don't leave without trying khinkali and khachapuri.
  • Stick to posted speed limits while driving — fines are strictly enforced.
  • Book flights and apply for your e-Visa at least 20 days ahead. Skip the Georgian visa entirely if you hold a valid US visa.
