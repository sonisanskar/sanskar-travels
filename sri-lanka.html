Day by Day

The Full Itinerary

Every stop, every cost, exactly how it happened.

Day 1

Arrival at Colombo & Journey to Bentota

  • Arrived at Colombo airport, took a tuk-tuk to Katunayake bus stop 300 LKR
  • Bus shuttle from Katunayake to Colombo Fort 320 LKR/person
  • Train ticket to Bentota, departing 2:30 pm 300 LKR
  • Reached Bentota by 4 pm, checked into hotel
  • Evening: sunset on the beach + turtle hatchery visit 2500 LKR/person
  • Quiet night in at the hotel
Day 2

Adventures at Bentota & Galle

  • Morning mangrove river boat safari 8000 LKR / boat (4-6 people)
  • Optional water sports — jet ski, paragliding
  • Bus to Galle from Aluthgama bus station 230 LKR/person
  • 15-min bus onward to Unawatuna 100 LKR/person
  • Overnight in Unawatuna, local nightlife
Day 3

Scuba Diving & Massage at Unawatuna

  • Scuba diving at Pearl Sea Divers $45/person
  • Cab to Galle 1000 LKR
  • Explored Galle Fort at sunset
  • Full body massage back in Unawatuna 3000 LKR/person

Galle Fort's iconic clock tower, right before sunset.

Day 4

Snorkeling at Mirissa

  • Early bus to Mirissa 150 LKR/person
  • Snorkeling with sea turtles & wildlife 5000 LKR
  • Sunset at Coconut Tree Hill
  • Optional surf lessons
  • Night out at Doctor's Club rave party
Day 5

Whale Watching & Travel to Ella

  • Whale watching tour, 5:30 am – 11:30 am $45
  • Afternoon bus to Ella via Matara, 4.5–5 hrs 800 LKR total
  • Evening cafes & party scene — a must-visit: "One Love" café
Day 6

Ella to Nuwara Eliya

  • Early tuk-tuk to Nine Arches Bridge 2000 LKR/tuk-tuk
  • 9:40 am scenic train to Nuwara Eliya 300 LKR/person
  • Pedro Tea Plantation & Sita Eliya (Ashok Vatika)
  • Sunset by the lake, night out at Thempekurada pub

The Nine Arches Bridge, early morning before the crowds.

Day 7

Nuwara Eliya to Kandy

  • 6 am train to Kandy, stunning landscapes 220 LKR/person
  • Visit Bahiravokanda Buddha statue on the hilltop
  • Tooth Relic Temple evening ceremony, starts 6:30 pm
Day 8

Last Day in Kandy & Colombo

  • 6:50 am train from Kandy to Colombo, arriving ~11 am 240 LKR/person
  • Afternoon rest — the heat hits hard
  • Evening: Gangaramaya Temple, Gallery Face One mall, sunset
  • Night at Bally's Casino — free membership at reception

💡 Travel Tips

  • Shift bases as early as possible in the morning to make the most of each day.
  • Bargain with local tuk-tuk drivers — quoted prices are rarely final.
  • Apply sunscreen regularly — the coastal sun tans fast.
  • Carry veg snacks if you're vegetarian — options can be limited outside cities.

💰 Trip Cost Summary

What the itinerary adds up to, per person — flight cost sourced from current India–Colombo fare averages since it wasn't in the original diary.

₹55,000 – ₹72,000 per person, all-in (~$660–$870)
Flights (round-trip, India ↔ Colombo)₹20,000 – ₹31,000
Stays (7 nights, budget–boutique)₹10,000 – ₹15,500
Local transport (trains, buses, tuk-tuks)₹2,800 – ₹4,400
Activities (turtle hatchery, safari, scuba, whale watching, massage)₹11,000 – ₹15,500
Food₹3,300 – ₹5,500
Visa (ETA) & misc₹2,200 – ₹3,300

Approximate figures for 2026 — actual cost depends on season, how far ahead you book flights, and whether you travel solo or split shared costs (tuk-tuks, boat safaris, hotel rooms) across a group. Sri Lanka is genuinely one of the cheapest tropical trips from India.
