Day by Day

The Full Itinerary

Every stop, every cost, exactly how it happened.

Day 1

Land in Hanoi

  • Bought a Viettel SIM at the airport and exchanged dollars for dong there (or find a gold jeweller in the city for a good rate)
  • Stayed at Central Backpackers Hostel Hanoi — best decision of the trip; they arranged airport pickup, arriving at our stay by 4 am
  • Rested 3–4 hours, then joined the hostel's city tour at 10 am
  • Roamed the streets of Hanoi for 3–4 hours, saw the lake, and tried local Vietnamese egg coffee
  • Watched the water puppet show at 4 pm — can be skipped, wasn't worth it
  • Happy hour back at the hostel — free beer, 7–8 pm
  • Hostel-organized games, dares and quizzes, then a pub crawl on the beer street, lively until 4 am

St. Joseph's Cathedral, spotted on the city tour.

Day 2

Halong Bay Cruise

  • Pre-booked the Halong Bay Hideaway cruise through the hostel, including an in-room jacuzzi
  • Bus departed at 8 am, switching a few ferries and buses to reach the cruise by 1 pm
  • Lunch on board, then a stop at a small island for sunbathing, a banana boat ride, and jumping off the boat terrace into the ocean
  • Sunset on the deck, followed by dinner, a DJ party, and happy hour on the cruise

The in-room jacuzzi view — best decision of the trip.

Day 3

Cruise Day Two & On to Ninh Binh

  • Morning kayaking session (~1 hour), then brunch and checkout
  • Arranged with the cruise to be dropped at Ninh Binh instead of back in Hanoi — arrived by 6 pm
  • Stayed at Tam Coc Ninh Binh Homestay; dinner at Indian restaurant Saffron by the river
  • Rented bikes from the homestay — most homestays here are very helpful with bookings

The crew at the island stop, before heading on to Ninh Binh.

Day 4

Explore the Ninh Binh Countryside

  • Rented scooters instead of a car for the day
  • 8 am: Trang An boat ride through the caves — a must-do, ~3 hours through some of the best landscapes; ask the boat driver for "route 3"
  • Lunch, then Hang Mua cave — trek to the summit for the best viewpoint (~2 hours round trip)
  • Onward to Hoa Lu ancient town, explored by rented bicycle — get there before 5 pm, the ticket counter closes
  • Back to the homestay, then an overnight bus to Phong Nha at 8:30 pm, arriving around 3 am

The boat ride through Trang An's caves and rice paddies.

Day 5

Conquer the Phong Nha Caves

  • Booked a day adventure — 4.5 km of kayaking and trekking into the caves with Phong Nha Discovery Tours, staying at their Discovery Lodge
  • Cave expedition began at 9 am after a briefing; reached the river bank by 10 am
  • Kayaked 2 km in complete darkness, guided only by helmet lights, then trekked 1–2 hours into the cave system
  • Took a dip and jumped into deep water at the mud bath; lunch served inside the cave itself
  • Explored ancient cave formations, back at the lodge by 4:30 pm
  • Sunset at the Son River, a massage, and dinner eaten with bamboo chopsticks
  • Overnight bus to Hoi An at 2:30 am (booked the day before through the stay), arriving around 10 am the next morning

Two kilometers of kayaking through the cave, guided only by helmet lights.

Day 6

The Ancient City of Hoi An

  • Stayed at Hoi An Corn Riverside — a great riverside property with a pool
  • Booked a coconut boat ride 250k VND/person and an evening lantern boat ride 100k VND/person through the host
  • Coconut boat tour included high-spinning stunts through the "bamboo forest"
  • Evening lantern ride dropped us at the Hoi An night market by 7 pm — great for shopping, bargain 70–80%
  • Club hopping at night (Velvet Club had a good atmosphere), back by 4 am

Lanterns and koi ponds — the Hoi An evening glow.

Day 7

Transfer to Da Nang

  • Taxi transfer to Da Nang arranged by the homestay owner; optional stop at Marble Mountain for a city view
  • Reached Alani Hotel by 2 pm; lunch at Flavours
  • Shopping at Han Market for first-copy sneakers and clothes — leave in time, it closes by 7–8 pm
  • Dinner near Han Khe Beach, then a whole-body massage at KOP Spa
  • Booked the Ba Na Hills trip through the hotel ~1.5 million VND/person
Day 8

Ba Na Hills

  • Full-day trip to Ba Na Hills from 8 am to 5 pm
  • Rest of the evening kept free
Day 9

Flight Home

  • Flights back to India

💡 Travel Tips

  • Buy a Viettel SIM and exchange currency at the airport, or find a gold jeweller in the city for good dong rates.
  • Hostels and homestays in Vietnam are exceptionally good at arranging tours, buses and pickups — let them handle logistics.
  • Book overnight buses a day in advance through your stay to lock in the best times.
  • Bargain hard at night markets — 70–80% off the asking price is normal.
  • The water puppet show in Hanoi is skippable if you're short on time.
